Our Story & Purpose

Founded with Love by a Dedicated Nutritionist

Health Shark is a Dhaka-based health food business, founded by a professional nutritionist with a simple yet timeless belief — Health is wealth.

We started with a focused mission: to serve secure, safe, and balanced food that helps people build immunity, stay energetic, and live healthier lives. We firmly believe that good health doesn't mean compromising on taste or convenience.

We use 100% fresh ingredients, natural hand-ground spices, and homemade wholesome recipes to bring you delicious treats, snacks, hearty family meals, and guilt-free desserts. Whether you are craving a quick healthy bite or ordering a wholesome pre-order feast for your family, we are here to serve you with care, hygiene, and uncompromising quality.

"To build immunity people need the right food in right amount. As a nutritionist I believe to serve people the secure and safe foods with the most dedicated team."

Farzana Wahab Founder & Nutritionist, Health Shark
